# MEGA PROMPT — COMPLETE CYBER INCIDENT REPORTING SYSTEM

You are an expert **full-stack developer, cybersecurity engineer, SOC architect, AI engineer, database architect, UI/UX designer, DevSecOps engineer, and QA engineer**.

Your task is to build a **complete, production-style, full-stack Cyber Incident Reporting, Tracking, Investigation, and Response Management System**.

The application name is:

# 🛡️ CyberShield

### Tagline:
**Report. Categorize. Track. Respond. Resolve. Stay Secure.**

---

# 1. OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT

Build a platform where organizations can:

- Report cybersecurity incidents
- Categorize incidents
- Prioritize incidents
- Calculate risk
- Assign incidents to security personnel
- Investigate incidents
- Collect and manage evidence
- Track incident status
- Communicate with stakeholders
- Escalate critical incidents
- Resolve incidents
- Close incidents
- Analyze incident trends
- Generate reports
- Maintain complete audit history

The central workflow must be:

```text
REPORT
   ↓
CATEGORIZE
   ↓
TRIAGE
   ↓
PRIORITIZE
   ↓
ASSIGN
   ↓
INVESTIGATE
   ↓
CONTAIN
   ↓
ERADICATE
   ↓
RECOVER
   ↓
RESOLVE
   ↓
CLOSE
   ↓
ANALYZE
```

Do NOT build this as a simple CRUD application.

Build it as a realistic **Security Operations / Incident Management platform**.

---

# 2. CORE PRODUCT VISION

Organizations often receive cyber incident reports through:

- Email
- Phone
- Chat applications
- Spreadsheets
- Manual forms
- Verbal communication

This causes:

- Delayed response
- Missing incidents
- Poor prioritization
- Lack of ownership
- Poor evidence management
- No centralized history
- Poor management visibility
- Difficult reporting

CyberShield must solve these problems through one centralized platform.
